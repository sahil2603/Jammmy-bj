Rules and payouts

  • 6 decks, shuffled fresh every round.
  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Wins pay 1 to 1. Ties push.
  • Dealer stands on all 17s and checks for blackjack when showing an Ace or a 10.
  • Double on any first two cards, also after a split.
  • Split once, any two cards of the same value. Split Aces get one card each.
  • When the dealer shows an Ace you can take insurance for half your bet. It pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.
  • No surrender. Minimum bet 10 coins, side bets from 1 coin.
  • Return to player: about 99.5% with perfect strategy (never take insurance). Turn on Jammmy AI to see it.

Provably fair

Before you bet, the next shuffle is locked in: you see a hash of the server's secret seed. Your client seed and the round number are mixed in, and after the round the secret seed is shown so you can rebuild the exact shuffle yourself.
